About THLRP

Tower Hamlets London RP is a UK-themed Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) roleplay community on Discord and Roblox. Realistic, organised and friendly, for players aged 13 and over.

🚓

ER:LC Roleplay

Immersive, realistic UK-style sessions in Liberty County.

🚒

Police, Fire & Civilian

Pick your department or play a civilian. There’s a role for everyone.

🛡️

Active Staff

A responsive team keeping sessions fair, safe and fun.

Friendly Community

New players welcome. Learn, make friends and have a laugh.
